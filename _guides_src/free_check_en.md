# Free AI visibility check, with no account and no credit card

AIVisib offers a free check that shows, live, whether AI assistants recommend your brand. You need neither an account nor a credit card: you enter your brand and your sector, and the check asks six real customer questions to ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity.

## What the check does

- It writes **six questions** a buyer in your sector would ask an AI.
- It asks them to **three engines**: ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity.
- It reads the **18 answers** and counts those that recommend your brand.

## What you get

- **A score**: the share of answers that recommend you, with its margin of error.
- **The competitors** the AIs name instead of you.
- **Two recommendations** to start with.

You can receive the result by email if you wish; it is not required.

## What the check does not do

It is a snapshot of 18 answers, not ongoing tracking. The full report measures your own questions on four engines (including Claude), three times each, every week, with an action plan. It starts at $59 per month.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to create an account?

No. The check runs from our home page, without signing up.

### Do I need a credit card?

No. No payment method is requested.

### In which languages does the check work?

In English, French and Arabic.

### Can I check a client's or a competitor's brand?

Yes. The check works for any brand.
