- **You want Claude and Gemini.** They are included in all our plans. At Otterly.AI they are paid add-ons, and Peec AI's entry plan is limited to three models.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is AIVisib cheaper than Otterly.AI?

With Gemini and Claude, yes. Otterly.AI Lite costs $29 for 15 questions on four engines; adding Gemini and Claude brings it to $67, against $59 at AIVisib for 25 questions, Gemini and Claude included.

### Is AIVisib cheaper than Peec AI?

At the entry level, yes: $59 against $95. Peec AI includes more questions (50) and measures daily; AIVisib includes all four engines and measures weekly.

### How much does Profound cost?

Profound does not publish a price: its website offers a free seven-day trial, then a plan on quote.

### Are there hidden costs at AIVisib?

No. There are no credits, no paid add-ons and no sales call. The only extra is an additional brand, at $49 per month on the Agency plan.

### Can I try it for free?

Yes. The [free check](/#audit) measures any brand from our home page, with no account and no credit card.
