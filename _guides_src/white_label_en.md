# White-label AI visibility reports for agencies

AIVisib lets an agency send its clients AI visibility reports under the agency's own name and logo. Each report measures whether ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude recommend the client's brand. White label is included from the Pro plan at $149 per month; the Agency plan, at $399 per month, covers eight clients.

## What carries your name

- **The online report**: your name and logo at the top, instead of AIVisib's.
- **The PDF** you export for a meeting or a proposal.
- **The share link**: a read-only page your client opens without creating an account, always showing the latest report.
- **The weekly email** sent automatically to each client, on the Agency plan.

Your client sees neither our name nor our interface. They see your work.

## What each report contains

- **The visibility score**: the share of AI answers that recommend the brand, with its margin of error.
- **The breakdown by engine**: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude, all included at no extra cost.
- **The breakdown by language**: English, French and Arabic.
- **The competitors** the AIs recommend instead of your client.
- **The sources** the AIs consult to answer.
- **An action plan**: three actions at most, each tied to a measured number.

Every question is asked three times to each engine, every week. The full method, limits included, is published in our [methodology](/methodology).

## Setting it up

1. Create one project per client: their brand, their competitors, the questions their buyers ask.
2. Add your agency's name and logo.
3. Run the first measurement: the report is ready in a few minutes.
4. Send the share link to your client, or let the weekly email go out on its own.
