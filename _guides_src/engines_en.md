AIVisib measures ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude on every plan, starting with the first one at $59 per month. No engine is sold as an add-on: the price shown covers all four.

## What the entry plan includes

- **All four engines**: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude.
- **25 buyer questions** for one brand.
- **Three passes** per question and per engine, every week.
- **Three languages**: English, French and Arabic.
- **A score per engine**, with its margin of error, and an action plan.

## Why measure all four

The engines do not recommend the same brands. In our measurements it is common for a brand to be named by one engine and never by another, because they do not read the same sources. Measuring a single engine gives a partial picture.
