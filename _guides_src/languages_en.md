# An AI visibility tool that works in Arabic and French, not only English

AIVisib measures a brand's visibility in AI answers in Arabic, French and English. The questions are asked in your customers' language, the answers are read in that language, and both the report and the interface are available in all three. It is included on every plan, from $59 per month.

## What is done in each language

- **The questions**: written and asked in Modern Standard Arabic, French or English.
- **Reading the answers**: the brand is recognised even when the AI writes it in Arabic letters.
- **The report**: analysis and action plan written in the reader's language.
- **The interface**: fully translated, with right-to-left display in Arabic.

## Why measure by language

The same brand can be recommended in English and absent in Arabic or French: the AIs do not rely on the same sources depending on the language of the question. The report therefore gives one score per language, and the number of questions lost in each. This gap is often the easiest gain to obtain, and you cannot see it without measuring it.

## Who it is for

- **Brands in the Gulf and North Africa**, whose customers ask their questions in Arabic or French.
- **French brands** that want to know what the AIs answer in French, not only in English.
- **Agencies** tracking clients in several countries, with reports under their own name in the client's language.
