# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

AIVisib ("we", "us"), a product operated by Norstrol LLC, helps businesses and agencies measure how AI assistants describe and recommend their brand. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what control you have. By using AIVisib you agree to it.

## 1. What we collect

- **Account data:** your name, email address and company name when you sign up or join the waitlist.
- **Tracking setup:** the brand names, competitor names, industry, languages and questions (prompts) you choose to monitor.
- **Results data:** the answers returned by AI engines for your prompts, and the metrics we derive from them.
- **Usage data:** basic technical information (browser, device, log data) to keep the service secure and reliable.
- **Free AI-visibility check:** the brand, sector and city you type, the result we measure, your email if you choose to give one, plus two technical items: the **country** your request comes from and a random **browser identifier**. We keep these two to prevent abuse of a free service and to know which countries use it. We do **not** store your IP address, and these items are never used to identify you personally or shared with anyone.
- **Privacy-friendly analytics:** we use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and understand traffic. It uses **no cookies**, no fingerprinting, and collects **no personal data**, which is why this site needs no cookie banner.
- **Payment data:** handled entirely by our payment processor. We never see or store your full card details.

## 2. How we use it

- To run your prompts against AI engines and build your visibility reports.
- To provide, secure, maintain and improve the service.
- To contact you about your account, reports and support requests.
- To process subscription payments.

## 3. What we do NOT do

- We do **not** sell your personal data, ever.
- We do **not** use your brand or competitor data for advertising.
- We do **not** share your results with other customers, including your competitors.

## 4. Third-party AI providers

To measure your visibility, AIVisib sends your monitored questions to third-party AI services (such as OpenAI, Google, Perplexity and Anthropic) through their official interfaces. These questions are public-style queries about your market; we do not send your personal data with them. Each provider processes them under its own terms.

## 5. Sharing

We share data only with the service providers who make AIVisib work (hosting, AI providers, payment processing, email delivery), under confidentiality obligations, or when required by law.

## 6. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time by emailing us. We will do so within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep records.

## 7. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections and restricted internal access.

## 8. Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and withdraw consent at any time. If you are in the EU, UK, or a region with similar laws (including GDPR), those rights apply to you and you may also lodge a complaint with your local authority.

## 9. Cookies and analytics

On this website (aivisib.com) we use **no cookies**. Traffic is measured with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies, no fingerprinting and collects no personal data, which is why you see no cookie banner. In the app (app.aivisib.com), we use only the storage strictly necessary to keep you signed in and keep the service secure. If we ever add cookie-based analytics or marketing, we will ask for your consent first.
